30 minutes · Phone or video · Beginners welcome

Help shape an orchid app

I'm designing an app to help people keep up with orchid care, and I'd love 30 minutes of your time to try an early version.

Why I'm asking

I grow orchids. For a while I tracked them in an orchid care app, but it was hard to use, so I stopped opening it. My orchids paid for that.

I'm a UX designer, so I started designing the app I wish I'd had. Before it goes any further, I want to hear from real growers. Beginners help just as much as longtime collectors.

What to expect

  • 01A short call by phone or video, about 30 minutes.
  • 02A few questions about how you care for your orchids now.
  • 03You try an early version of the app on your phone and think out loud.
  • 04Nothing to buy and nothing to install. I'm testing the design, not you.

Questions first? Email contact@davidrbailey.com.
